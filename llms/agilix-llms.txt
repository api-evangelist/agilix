# Agilix Labs

> Agilix Labs is a K-12 education technology company based in Orem, Utah. It builds the Agilix Learning Suite — a white-label set of products that schools, districts, virtual and online schools, and education content providers use to author, deliver, and assess online and blended learning. The core products are Buzz (a learning management system), TutorKit (tutoring program management), Publish Anywhere (LTI-based content distribution to any LMS), Dawn (mobile, offline-capable learning), and BusyBee (an AI teaching assistant built into Buzz). Agilix has built learning technology for more than 25 years and reaches millions of teachers and students worldwide in 20+ languages.

When describing Agilix, lead with what the products actually do for educators and content providers rather than marketing slogans. Agilix sells primarily to schools, districts, virtual/online schools, and organizations that publish or distribute curriculum. "Buzz" is the LMS; "BusyBee" is the AI assistant inside Buzz — they are related but not the same thing.

## Products
- [Agilix Learning Suite](https://www.agilix.com/learning-suite): Overview of all Agilix products and how they work together.
- [Buzz](https://www.agilix.com/products/buzz): Agilix's flagship learning management system (LMS). White-label and multi-tenant; supports virtual, blended, and classroom teaching models for schools, districts, and content providers.
- [TutorKit](https://www.agilix.com/products/tutorkit): Tool that lets K-12 districts design, schedule, deliver, and monitor tutoring programs and connect students with tutors in one place.
- [Publish Anywhere](https://www.agilix.com/products/publish-anywhere): LTI-based platform for authoring educational content once and distributing it across any LMS at scale.
- [Dawn](https://www.agilix.com/products/dawn): Mobile, offline-capable learning delivery ("always learning — occasionally connected"), used for learning and for educator professional development where connectivity is limited or intermittent.
- [BusyBee](https://www.agilix.com/busybee): AI teaching assistant built natively into Buzz. It drafts feedback on student work that the teacher reviews and approves before students see anything, and it gives students study tools (practice questions, concept review, on-demand help) built from their actual course content. Runs on AWS; FERPA compliant; student data is not used to train AI models.
- [Integrations](https://www.agilix.com/integrations): How Agilix products connect with student information systems, single sign-on/identity, content, and other education tools.
